A SMOOTHER SWITCH

Help customers
get mobile-ready.

Keep the setup simple. Start with their phone number and the phone they want to use.

Applies to both models
01Keep their current phone number+

Moving a number to a new mobile service is called a number transfer (or “port”).

  1. Ask the customer to get their account number and any required transfer PIN from their current provider.
  2. Check that the account name, billing details, and number are correct.
  3. Keep their current service active until the transfer is complete. Confirm any transfer protection requirements with their current provider.

Transfer requirements and timing vary by provider. Share account details securely, not through this preview.

02Check whether their phone will work+
  1. Check compatibility. Use the phone model and IMEI (its device ID) to confirm it is supported on the selected service.
  2. Check the carrier lock. A locked phone may need to be unlocked by the current provider before switching. Being unlocked does not by itself confirm compatibility.
  3. Check the SIM type. An eSIM is digital; a physical SIM is a removable card. Confirm which option the phone and selected service support.

Confirm compatibility and activation requirements before promising service.

03eSIM or physical SIM—what’s the difference?+

eSIM: a digital SIM installed on a supported phone.

Physical SIM: a small card inserted into the phone.

The right choice depends on the device and mobile service. Check both before activation.
